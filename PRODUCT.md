# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Project owners, developers, and main contractors** evaluating a Quantity Surveyor / cost engineer for budget control, tender documents, and final accounts.
- **HRD and directors at construction firms (BUMN, multinational private)** assessing technical competence, software fluency, and project track record.
- **Architects and structural engineers** looking for a cost-side collaborator on design-and-build or tender work.

## Product Purpose

A high-end personal branding site for **Aldi Rifaldi, S.T.** that proves precision in Quantity Surveying and construction cost management through project evidence, cost metrics, and a systematic end-to-end cost lifecycle narrative. Success means a qualified visitor understands the specialization within seconds and contacts Aldi or downloads the executive CV.

## Positioning

A civil engineer who operates as a commercial strategist: quantity take-off and BIM 5D measurement joined to value engineering, contract administration, and final-account settlement. The site demonstrates the mechanism with a live cost-breakdown dossier instead of only claiming it.

## Operating Context

Pre-contract estimating (RAB/OE/RAP), AHSP/SNI pricing, tender documents and BoQ, sub-contractor tender equalization, monthly opname and progress certificates (MC-0 to MC-100), variation orders (VO/CCO), material waste audits, cost monitoring and cashflow forecasting, value engineering, and final account settlement. Tools referenced: Glodon Cubicost (TAS/TRB), Autodesk Revit (BIM 5D), AutoCAD, advanced Excel, MS Project / Primavera P6.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-page portfolio with: hero and quick metrics; about and QS philosophy; competency matrix and software stack; filterable featured projects; interactive BoQ / cost-breakdown preview; timeline and qualifications; contact; print-ready executive CV. Static front end, no form backend. Contact channel is LinkedIn only. Project metrics in the supplied source documents are explicitly representative sample data, not verified client claims. Employer names, employment dates, university name, and certification numbers were not supplied.

## Brand Commitments

Name: **Aldi Rifaldi, S.T.** LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/aldirifaldi13/. Headline: "Precision in Every Volume. Excellence in Every Structure." Visual world pinned by the supplied design guidelines: Architectural Industrial Luxury / Precision Blueprint; obsidian concrete, charcoal slate, brushed gunmetal, structural steel lines, alabaster text, brass/gold accent (#C5A880); Cormorant Garamond or Playfair Display display accents, Plus Jakarta Sans body, JetBrains Mono for data; architectural grid, crosshair markers, glass slate cards, brass hover edges.

## Evidence on Hand

- `brand_copywriting_content_architecture.md` — profile copy, three pillars, three sample projects with representative metrics.
- `design_system_brand_guidelines.md` — palette, typography, ornaments, micro-interactions.
- No real project photography, client logos, testimonials, CV PDF, or verified career dates. Those must not be fabricated; the CV is generated from supplied copy and is print-ready in the browser.

## Product Principles

1. Precision is the brand: every number, label, and alignment should read as measured, not decorated.
2. Show the cost mechanism, not just the title; the BoQ preview is evidence of how Aldi works.
3. Luxury through restraint: brass is an accent, never a wash; hierarchy comes from scale, rules, and space.
4. Respect the visitor's time: the specialization, value, and action are legible in the first viewport.
5. Never invent commercial proof: representative data stays identified, missing career facts stay absent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable navigation, filters, tabs, and expandable cost rows; visible focus states; reduced-motion support; semantic headings, tables, and labels; contrast meeting WCAG AA for body and large text.
